- D1 · 2026-11-14 · 67563a27 · agent · The harmonization ideas are checked on real FIRMS archive hotspots for Ukraine (per-country yearly CSVs, vegetation fires `type = 0`, monthly totals, factors learned on some years and tested on the others) before any design is proposed. Why: To check the ideas on live data; the agent announced it mid-turn and the person let the work go on.
- D2 · 2026-11-14 · 67563a27 · both · In the roadmap of the harmonization, the not-a-fire filter is stage 2 and the common table of points is stage 3: non-fire points are removed first, set aside in a separate table (not deleted) so the static-source map can be built from them, and one rule applies to all years (a point on the static-source map is not a fire, whether archive or fresh). Why: The person said the not-a-fire data must be discarded as the first step; the agent agreed and added that non-fires are set aside, not erased, and that the rule is the same for archive and fresh points.
- D3 · 2026-11-14 · 67563a27 · agent · The unit of the harmonized series is VIIRS points (MODIS is scaled up to VIIRS), the step is the month, Ukraine first and then the world, and the storage is Parquet plus DuckDB. Why: The future is in VIIRS; daily counts swing by a factor of two; the Ukraine data is already at hand and checks fast; the world archive has hundreds of millions of rows that pure Python cannot handle. The person did not object.
- D4 · 2026-11-14 · 67563a27 · agent · The training years for MODIS-to-VIIRS factors are bounded by data, not by '2021': a year is usable while the overpass local time is within 10 minutes of the usual one, which is Aqua up to 2022 and Terra up to 2021; VIIRS 2016-2021 and NOAA-20 2018-2021 were downloaded to extend the spike to ten years. Why: The Ukraine archive shows Aqua at 12:52-13:01 local solar time for 2001-2022 and 13:14 in 2023, so the agent corrected its earlier 'not after 2021'. The person did not object.
- D5 · 2026-11-14 · 6083b752 · both · The cornerstone of the project: hotspot counts of different sensors are never put side by side as they are; before any chart, series or calendar joins them, the counts of the older sensor (MODIS) are converted to the VIIRS scale by the harmonization multipliers. The proof is evidence/why-harmonize/index.html: over Ukraine (type = 0) MODIS alone shows 2011 -> 2012 as -30%, MODIS up to 2011 stitched to VIIRS from 2012 shows +87%, and the stitched 2012-2025 mean is 2.5 times the 2001-2011 mean where MODIS alone shows -20%; the harmonized series (Aqua x monthly multiplier up to 2011, VIIRS after) shows -40%, the right direction. Why: The person named it the cornerstone of the whole task: without it the burning calendar shows the change of satellite instead of the fires, every year after 2012 above the norm and every year before it below.
